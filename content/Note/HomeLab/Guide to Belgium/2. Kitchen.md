Your landlord should prepared the kitchenette for you. It's not Germany here. 
What you should be expected in your kitchen (most of the time it's all from ikea)
- 2 small electrical stoves
- a small sinks
- a pair of knives
- a cutting board (might be flexi) https://www.ikea.com/be/en/p/finfoerdela-bendable-chopping-board-green-bright-green-40559680/
- Pan and pot
- Dishes and bowl
- A few glasses and tea cups
- Cutlery: knife, fork, spoon, coffee spoon.. (NO CHOPSTICKS)
Note: Bring your chopsticks, it's 2 eu/5 chopstick here, spend your money wiser

It depend a lot on your cooking style. Here is a list of suggestions you should buy for your kitchen pantry:
- Chopsticks: go to asian store
- A vegetable peeler (Cheap one here is 3 eu, expensive one is 10 eu)
- Scissors (it's about 7 eu for a kitchen scissor)
- Soup spoon. you might need a ladle or a slotted spoon for soup.
- Small bowl: if you need to eat rice from a small bowl, get some at asian store. It's actually not too expensive, about 2 eu/one.
- Paper towels (about 5 eu/pack of 3)
- A favorite cups or mugs. If you drinks beer, buy a beer glass, it's like 2 eu. If you drink tea, buy a tea mug, it's about 3 - 4 eu. If you drink protein shaker, please bring your own shaker or you could buy 1 at Kruidvat or Hema for about 6 eu. In short, buy a favorite cup, it is very important for your mood. 
- Plastic container: for food prepping, always buy at Action, it will save you tons
- Glass container: similarly, check for Action first, then Amazon. You can get good quality Pyrex here, but it's very heavy and not suitable for studio life.
