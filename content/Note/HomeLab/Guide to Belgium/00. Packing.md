### General
The packing guide 
### 1. Electronic
You should have 3 cubes for the electronic. 
- The 1st one is for your laptop
- The 2nd one is for charger and adapter
- The 3rd one is for tools
#### Laptop
- The school could provide you with laptop, but it has a French keyboard, which is terrible. 
	- If you plan to have 1, contact your school asap and bring a bluetooth keyboard.
	- You should always consider you have this option in case of bad weather
- Laptop should be put into a case with accessories
	- Charger.
	- Adapter (HDMI ?)
	- Adapter for internet (buying separately from above adapter would cost you less and most of the time you won't using both functions at the same time)
	- Presentation pen
	- Spare usb.
- You won't be able to repair your laptop here, plan accordingly for the worst. A few thing you could prepare, listing from stupid to very arduous
	- Bring 2 laptop (stupid)
	- Bring 1 spare equipment that can be exchange for laptop - eg. a Tablet
	- Contact the school for a laptop (in French)
	- Learn to repair a laptop by yourself

	- 5 pairs of underwear

- Base layer
	- 3 - 4 bases of your choice

- Pants
	- 2 pants max
		- 1 jean
		- 1 dress pant

### Sleeping clothes

- 2 sets of pajama

### Winter clothes:
- Middle layers: 2 - 3 items
	- 2 sweaters
		- 1 made from fleeces
		- 1 made from natural wool
- Shell: 
	- 1 Jacket

	- 1 rain coat or rain poncho
		- Umbrella will not works well in heavy wind weather, it depends a lot on where you live.
		- No rain defense in your inventory = sudden death

### Hand
- Buy 1 pair of wool gloves in Vietnam and that's it
- If your hands are cold
	- Buy a gloves with mitt on tops (2 layer gloves)
		- Put a thermal pad in the mitt, it's awesome 
	- Put the thermal pad in your jacket pocket, it's not bad.

### Knee
- I am not using it so this is not from my experience
- It's strange but a lot of ppl swear on having a knee protection 
- If you have a cold leg, buy 1 pair of knee protection in the sport store 
- Wear it outside of your base.
### Socks:
- At least 5 pairs of socks
- You might want to double socks, buy outer socks in 1 size higher, you can put thermal pad here too.
	- Keep in mind that double socks require a bigger shoes, which is not always available.
- Material: 
	- Synthetic or wool
		- Wool sock is awesome, but it's expensive and most likely thicker than your normal socks which lead to the requirement of bigger shoes
	- Avoid high cotton content sock
		- it's actually very hard to find 100% cotton sock, the sock would be broken, shrink in weird way and very easy to be tear. 
	


## 3. Medication
If you have prescription, please packing them in at least 2 places (1 in your checked luggage and 1 in your cabin luggage). It's very important. You might need doctor's confirmation for your condition to bring special medication, please contact the airlines beforehand. Glass is a prescription too :)), you might want to have 2 glasses, just in case. Because the glass price in eu is about 400 eu/one.

Drugs in general needs to be packed in a clear through package so that the airport custom can check them. A cheap ziplock bag, clear plastic boxes or specialized clear packing bags. Liquid need to be stored in small bottles around 30 - 50 mL, you can bring multiple bottles. 

A small first aid kit should be prepared, some items I recommend in your kits:
- Urgo bandage in 2 sizes small and large (the large one can be use like an gauze in emergency)
- Hand wash gel (or alcohol patches)
- Eye drops (you can use this emergency water to clean your wound if the cap is not opened yet)
- Nail clipper (new one in EU is around 3 -5 eu)
- an emergency scissor (you won't know when you might need one)

- Common drugs list:
	- Vitamin D: It's winter here, it's very very important (should not buy multivitamin, just D3. The story of A - D - K  and magnesium is a very controversial topic. I'm sure that taking D3 is good in winter here, but mixing with other vitamins ? I'm not so sure anymore. Also ultradosing of D3 - like 10000U/daily is not recommended.)
	- Lip balm for moisturize. Join our cult of vaseline, please.
	- Paracetamol: choose 1 type that work best for you (some ppl swear on the pill, some prefer tablet, branding most likely won't matter much)
	- Oresol: for dehydration or after sickness recovery
	- Car sickness pill
	- If you are a girl, buy *the pill* + Iron supplement too. Better prepare for the scenario.
- You can buy almost everything in this section in Belgium so don't overpack. 

## 4. Papers
List of required paper to bring with you:
- Passport
- A few passport size picture (3 - 5)
- Vietnamese ID
- Student acceptance letter
- Rental agreement/confirmation of residence

When you got to Belgium, ask your International Cooperation Unit or equivalent bureau for the next steps. 
- In case you are staying less than 90 days in Belgium, ask them about the Declaration of Arrival.
- In case you are staying more, ask for the Residence Permit.
