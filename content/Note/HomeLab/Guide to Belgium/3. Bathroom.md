## Bathroom in Belgium (Salle de bain)
- Typical studio bathroom is very small  0.8 x 2.5m, equipment
	- a shower
	- a toilet
	- handwasher with mirror.
	- A water heater 
		- always as for the water heater, their location, capacity...
		- In Namur, it's a Bulex with a capacity of 50L, it's so big and cost so much space. However, there is not enough for more than 10 minutes shower.
	- A toilet brush (maybe)
- You are required to buy the essentials:
	- Brushes
	- Paper
	- Soap
	- Shower gel
	- Accessories

## Toiletry
1. Brushes 
	- You need to have at least 2 brushes for the bathroom
		- 1 for toilet
		- 1 for cleaning the handwasher
	- About 2 - 5 euro per brush
	- WTB: Action
2. Paper (Toiletpapier)
	- You could pack toilet papers with you, 4 rolls is enough for 3 months
	- The prices varies a lot from 2 - 6 eu/packs of 6 rolls
	- Don't be cheap with toilet paper, never buy the cheapest one. 
	- But I don't review toilet paper so I don't know the best one. I am using Boni (https://www.colruyt.be/nl/producten/31647)
	- WTB: ?? (I bought at colruyt)
3. Toilet cleaner:
	- Toilet cleaner is very cheap in Belgium, at 1 - 2 eu/bottle. Get 1 asap
	- Famous brand included Mr, Clean or Le Canard (con vịt).
	- I use them more often than in Vietnam, for I don't have a bidet now.
	- Toilet got stuck ? Ask you landlord for help, they should resolve this
		- If you are the type that take the matter to your own hands, you can buy plunger chemical at Kruidvat (cheaper) or in any supermarket. A small convenient store might lack the products. Price is steep from 5 - 10 eu/bottler of 500 - 700 mL. Do they work? let's hope they do. Most of the time they unclog by dissolving hair, fat and protein. If your toilet is clog by other causes, then it won't work. Research before buying.
4. Soap (Savon)
	- You should buy 1 for hand and 1 for bath
	- Handsoap:
		- Very easy to buy anywhere
		- It's around 1 - 2 eu/bottle of 500 mL
		- Very cheap (maybe because of covid)
		- WTB: Kruidvat, Delhalze
	- Bath soap
		- I like to use bar, and it's harder to find in belgium
		- It's cheap at 2 eu/pack of 4 bars
		- Premium brand like Savon de Marseille is worth a try, it's about 5 - 10 eu/bar of 300 grams. Locals also sell soaps.
		- You can also pack your soap, but I think you should try the soaps here.
		- WTB: anywhere, local brand ?
5. Shower gel
	- You should bring your shower gel from Vietnam. European hair is built different, don't risk it. 
	- Also, water in Europe is hard. Thus, you need to use shower gel, nopoo is impossible here. Trust me, I break my 2 years streak of nopoo here.
	- The price is varied crazily, but expect up to 30 eu for a combo of shower + softener here. I am cheap so I go to Kruidvat and buy their 2 eu shower gel. However, anyone who care about their hair should not even try to test out the European hair care. Bring your own.
	- WTB: Vietnam. (or Kruidvat if you don't give a shit)
## Cosmetic
I am not good with cosmetic but I think you should not buy your skin care routine in europe. Once again, their products are tailored for Europeans and would not works as well as your trusted product. So bring your own cosmetics. 
- Prepare for chapped lips and dry hand (xước măng rô - agnail). A large pot of vaseline is like 5 eu here, don't bother with anything other than the pure 100% petrolatum. Bring a pumice stone or similar for removing dead skin and you are golden. (https://www.kruidvat.be/nl/search?text=vaseline).
- Perfume: mind your perfume here, some could smell bad to local. I'm suck at this but just ask.
