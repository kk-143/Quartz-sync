## What does it do
The program import a video file
Output:
- 1 txt data file of Contour area 
- 1 simple line plot of the total area
## How it's work
The video was converted to 8-bit black and white and a Threshold was applied.
Threshold method applied was Li, it was the one which performed the best out of all the methods in ImageJ.
A series of Erodes and Dilates were applied to remove debris pixels from the videos
Analyze particle is used to extract the largest mask in the image as well as the area of the mask.
Data was plotted and saved with built-in function of imageJ.
